DATA & PRIVACY

Privacy Notice

Last updated: 2 November 2026

We aim to collect only information needed to support Indian Market Lab's optional beta features. Please read this notice before entering an email address or sending feedback.

Optional signups are closed

We are not currently accepting email waitlist registrations or in-site feedback submissions. Stock research and paper-trading tools remain available without creating an account.

1. About the service

Indian Market Lab operates this educational market-research website. The service currently does not offer public accounts, brokerage access, or paid memberships. Email registration is not offered during this preview. Users can explore the research tools without signing up.

2. Information processed

Browsing and research: when you use a chart, search for an instrument or view a page, the service processes the request and its technical details to deliver the requested content. Hosting providers may record IP addresses, request times and device/browser information for reliability and security.

Paper Portfolio: saved paper-trading ideas currently stay in your browser's local storage. They are not synced to an account and may be visible to other people using the same browser profile.

Beta interest and feedback: when these features become available, any optional signup will request an email address, a feature interest and an affirmative choice to receive beta updates. Feedback may include the selected feature, rating and message. Neither form is presently accepting submissions.

3. How information is used

Information is processed to provide chart displays, stock research, user-requested calculations, service stability and security. If optional beta signup is opened, the contact details supplied with consent will be used for the beta messages described at the time of signup, not for brokerage orders or unrelated advertising.

4. Service providers and external links

Indian Market Lab's application is hosted on Render, whose infrastructure may process connection and request information. The Trader Terminal uses TradingView Lightweight Charts for chart rendering. Its JavaScript is currently served through the unpkg content delivery network, which may receive the connection IP address and request details; using the library does not mean TradingView supplies or verifies our market quotes. Stock-history and news requests can involve third-party services, which have their own data-use practices. Following a news link takes you to its publisher's website and privacy terms.

Where optional beta storage is enabled in the future, information will be held by contracted cloud-service providers with access limited to authorized operations. We do not currently operate a public email subscriber database.

5. Choices and consent

Use of the stock research tools does not require giving us your email address. Future invitations or updates will be optional and subject to affirmative consent. Before opening subscriptions, the Platform will provide a verified privacy contact and a way to request withdrawal or deletion.

6. Storage, retention and security

Paper Portfolio entries are stored on your own device until you remove them or clear browser data. Technical service logs may be held by our hosting and external service providers according to their respective retention practices. We use reasonable safeguards for Platform operations, but no internet service can guarantee absolute security.

Any future stored signup data will be covered by a stated retention and deletion policy before collection begins.

7. Applicable privacy rights

Privacy and data-protection obligations depend on applicable Indian law, including the Digital Personal Data Protection framework as its provisions come into effect. If future collection is enabled, information about applicable requests, contacts and response procedures will be published here.

8. Questions and complaints

Public email registration is not currently available. A verified contact for data questions and complaints will be published before the service begins collecting email signups. Until then, please avoid including personal or financial-account information in any public issue or feedback channel.

9. Updates

We may update this notice when functionality, third-party services or information practices change. The date at the top indicates the current version.
