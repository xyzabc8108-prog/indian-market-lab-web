DATA & PRIVACY

Privacy Notice

Last updated: 02/11/2026

We aim to collect only information needed to support Indian Market Lab's optional beta features. Please read this notice before entering an email address or sending feedback.

Beta privacy readiness

Waitlist collection and in-site feedback storage are disabled until a separate persistent database and a verified privacy contact are configured and tested. The public website will indicate whether forms are available. Please do not submit sensitive or financial account information.

1. Who operates this platform?

The website is branded Indian Market Lab. Verified legal operator identity, a privacy contact and a formal grievance contact have not yet been published. These details must be provided before the site solicits personal data from public beta users. No contact email is invented in this policy.

2. What data may be processed?

Stock searches and charts: requests include selected securities, history ranges and the minimum technical request information necessary to serve a page. External quote and news services may process their own request information in accordance with their policies. Hosting providers can keep operational or security logs.

Optional waitlist (when enabled): an email address, interested feature, and consent timestamp may be stored to send the beta updates the person expressly requested. Marketing subscriptions are not automatic.

Optional feedback (when enabled): selected feature, a 1–5 satisfaction rating, a feedback message and creation timestamp may be stored. Do not include sensitive information in free-text feedback.

Paper journal: saved paper-trade notes currently remain in the browser's local storage; they are not a private cloud account. Anyone with access to the same browser profile may be able to view them.

3. Why is data used?

Data is used to operate pages, perform requested calculations, prevent spam, respond to optional feedback and—only with opt-in consent—send beta invitations or updates. The website does not currently operate a paid service or broker account.

4. Storage and service providers

When configured, optional beta submissions are intended to be stored in a separate private Supabase database, with its secret access key held on the backend rather than exposed to visitors. Until storage is operational, submissions are not accepted. Hosting, data feeds and browser services may process limited request information in providing their functionality. Public GitHub issues are visible to others and should not be used for private details.

5. Consent and withdrawal

Joining the waitlist is optional and requires an active consent checkbox. We plan to provide a contact channel for consent withdrawal and deletion requests before accepting real emails. Until the channel and secure storage are configured, the waitlist remains closed.

6. Retention and safeguards

The retention period for future waitlist data, the responsible operator, a contact address, and a deletion workflow must be finalized before the forms open publicly. The present backend includes validation and basic abuse throttling, but these do not eliminate every security risk. Browser-local notes can be cleared through browser storage settings. External service and hosting logs may have separate retention policies.

7. Legal framework and rights

Applicable Indian privacy laws may provide rights and obligations depending on processing and their effective commencement dates. The Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023 and Rules, 2025 have phased commencement. This notice does not claim all provisions are already in force or that merely providing this page ensures compliance.

8. Privacy contact

A verified privacy and grievance contact has not been published for this beta. Do not enable real-email collection until this is corrected. No personal details or contact credentials should be posted publicly in issue threads.

9. Changes

We may revise this notice as the beta's data collection, ownership and storage evolve. Updated text will show its revision date and should be reviewed before enabling new categories of personal-data collection.
