DATA & PRIVACY

Privacy Notice

Last updated: November 2, 2026

We collect only the minimum information needed to operate Indian Market Lab and its optional beta features. Please read this notice before submitting an email address or platform feedback.

Privacy Summary

Indian Market Lab processes standard technical request data to render market charts and stores your email address or feedback only when you voluntarily submit it through an available form. Paper-trading journal entries remain in your browser. Please do not submit passwords, brokerage credentials or sensitive financial account details through this website.

1. Who operates this platform?

Indian Market Lab is an educational market research and paper-trading platform operated by Rohan Chhaya, based in Rajkot, Gujarat, India. For privacy inquiries, data requests or grievances, use the verified contact details in Section 8 when the contact channel is available.

2. What data may be processed?

Stock searches and charts: Requests include selected stock symbols, historical date ranges and technical request metadata such as IP addresses and browser user-agent information needed to deliver page content. Market-data providers, content delivery networks and hosting services may also process standard connection metadata under their own policies.

Optional waitlist: When registration is open, joining voluntarily provides your email address, selected feature interest and consent timestamp to send the Indian Market Lab beta updates you requested. We do not automatically enrol you in third-party marketing lists. Waitlist registration is currently closed.

Optional feedback: When the form is open, voluntarily submitted feedback includes a feature category, satisfaction rating (1–5), message and submission timestamp. Do not include sensitive personal or financial data in feedback. In-site feedback submissions are currently closed.

Paper journal: Your saved paper-trading notes and virtual preferences are held in your browser's local storage (localStorage) rather than a cloud user account. Other people using the same device and browser profile may be able to access them.

3. Why is data used?

Information is processed to render historical charts, run user-initiated statistical calculations, maintain service security, reduce automated abuse, evaluate optional feedback and—with your active consent—send requested beta invitations or platform updates. Indian Market Lab does not operate brokerage accounts or process real-money securities orders.

4. Storage and service providers

When optional waitlist and feedback services are enabled, voluntarily submitted information is stored in an access-controlled cloud database using server-side authentication and validation. Website hosting is provided by Render, and the Trader Terminal uses TradingView Lightweight Charts™, with chart-library scripts delivered through an external content delivery service. Hosting and delivery providers may process limited technical request information, including IP addresses and request metadata. TradingView Lightweight Charts is a chart-rendering library, not a provider of verified market quotes for this Platform.

External market-data and news sources may process requests according to their respective privacy practices. Following an external news link takes you to that publisher's website.

5. Consent and withdrawal

Joining any beta waitlist is optional and requires an affirmative opt-in. Once email registration is available, you may withdraw consent for further beta communications or request deletion of your voluntarily submitted contact details through the privacy contact listed below.

6. Retention and safeguards

When waitlist collection becomes available, contact details are intended to be retained for the beta program or until consent is withdrawn, subject to legally required retention. Feedback will be retained only as necessary to evaluate product improvements. We aim to address verified deletion requests within 14 business days of receipt, subject to legal obligations and identity verification. No online service is completely risk-free.

Paper-trading notes can be removed by clearing this website's browser storage. Browser data may also disappear if storage is cleared or the device is changed.

7. Legal framework and rights

Under applicable Indian privacy and data-protection law, including provisions of the Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023 as they come into force, you may have rights to request information about personal data held by us, seek correction or erasure, withdraw consent for future communications and raise a grievance. The availability and handling of each request depend on applicable law and any lawful retention requirements.

8. Privacy & Grievance Contact

Operator / Grievance Contact: Rohan Chhaya

Location: Rajkot, Gujarat, India

Email: Our verified contact email will be available before beta registrations open.

Once the contact channel opens, we aim to acknowledge privacy inquiries within 48 hours and process verified deletion requests within 14 business days, unless applicable law requires a different timeline.

9. Changes

We may update this notice as the Platform's features and data practices evolve. Changes will be reflected on this page with an updated revision date before introducing new categories of personal-data collection.
